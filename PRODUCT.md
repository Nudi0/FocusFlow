# FocusFlow — Product Overview

## Purpose
FocusFlow is a high-craft personal productivity workspace, gamified quest engine, and Apple Health telemetry dashboard designed specifically for **Alanoud Alwetaid**.

## Core Audience & Identity
- **Owner**: Alanoud Alwetaid
- **Aesthetic**: Girly Gamer / Kawaii Cyber-Luxe (Soft rose pastels, midnight amethyst dark mode, crystal glass cards, and gamer accent glows).
- **Tone**: Playful, encouraging, organized, and focused on momentum.

## Primary Modules
1. **Command Center (Overview)**:
   - Live momentum tracker and real-time clock.
   - Quick Quest capture and daily priority feed.
   - Live Activity Stream logging wins, pomodoros, and sync events.
   - Multi-format data export (Excel `.xlsx`, Word `.docx`, and CSV).
2. **iPhone Apple Health Live Telemetry**:
   - Webhook & iOS Shortcut synchronization for Screen Time, Daily Steps, Workout minutes, and Sleep Duration.
   - One-click copy for personal iOS Shortcut URLs.
3. **Daily Quests & Task Management**:
   - Filterable quest engine (All, Pending, Completed).
   - Priority and category tagging with craft-floor accessible badge contrast.
   - Custom dynamic section creation (`+ Add New Section`).
4. **Pomodoro Gamer Pod**:
   - Focus session timer with presets (25m Focus, 5m Rest, 15m Long Break).
   - Streak counters and celebration particle bursts on session completion.
5. **Analytics & Reflection**:
   - Weekly productivity trends and completion velocity.
